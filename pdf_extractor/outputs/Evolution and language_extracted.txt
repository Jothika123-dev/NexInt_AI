• India   is   a   diverse   nation,   and   this   diversity   is   reflected   in   
the   country's numerous scripts and languages. 
• India's script and language development is a fascinating journey that 
goes back to ancient times.  
• The Indian subcontinent has a plethora of scripts, including Brahmi, 
Kharosthi, Devanagari, Tamil, and others, that contribute to its rich 
linguistic heritage.  
• The Indus script,  the   oldest  of the   early   Indian   scripts, was  
primarily pictographic. 
• The Indus Valley Civilization, which flourished around 2600 BCE, is 
the source of this script. However, scholars are still attempting to 
comprehend the meaning and purpose of the Indus script, which has 
yet to be deciphered.

❖ The   Tamil   script,   which   is   one   of   India's   oldest   scripts,   is   
another significant script. 
 
❖ Since at least the third century BCE, Tamil literature has been rich. 
 
❖ The Tamil content is one of a kind in that it has a different      
arrangement of characters for each consonant-vowel mix.  
 
❖ Because of this, it is more difficult to  master than other scripts and is 
more complex Indian   language   development   has   also   been   
fascinating. 
 
❖ There   are numerous languages spoken in India, each with its own 
distinct history and  development.
